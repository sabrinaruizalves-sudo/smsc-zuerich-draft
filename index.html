01 — WHO WE ARE

Medicine at its Limits explores the boundaries of modern medicine and the challenges of healthcare in extreme environments with a particular emphasis on four key domains:

  • Deep Space
  • Deep Sea
  • High Altitude
  • Emergency Medicine
01

Deep Space

The human body in weightlessness, radiation and isolation on long missions.

02

Deep Sea

Pressure, cold and isolation: diving medicine under extreme environmental conditions.

03

High Altitude

Thin air, hypoxia and the limits of acclimatisation, from base camp to the summit.

04

Emergency Medicine

Decisions made in seconds, when every minute counts.

Discover new perspectives, challenge conventional thinking, and expand your understanding of medicine beyond the boundaries of traditional clinical practice. Connect with fellow medical students, engage with leading experts, and explore the research and innovations shaping the future of healthcare.
